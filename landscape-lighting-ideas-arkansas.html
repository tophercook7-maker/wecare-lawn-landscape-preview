Landscape lighting ideas for Arkansas homes

Good outdoor lighting is one of the highest-impact, lowest-cost ways to transform a yard — it doubles your evening use of the space, boosts curb appeal, and adds real safety. Here are the ideas that make the biggest difference on Central Arkansas properties.

Uplight your best features

A warm light aimed up the trunk of a mature tree, a stone wall, or the front of the house creates depth and drama after dark. Uplighting your two or three best features does more for the look of a property at night than a dozen scattered fixtures.

Light the paths and steps

Low path lights and step lights are about safety first — no more feeling your way up dark steps — but they also gently define walkways and lead the eye through the yard. This is the lighting that pays for itself in peace of mind.

Wash the patio and gathering spots

Soft, indirect light around a patio, pergola, or seating area turns it into a place you'll actually use on a summer night. The trick is to keep it warm and low — enough to see and feel cozy, not floodlit.

Feature the water and stonework

If you have (or are planning) a water feature, patio, or carved-concrete work, a light or two placed just right makes it a centerpiece at night. Since we design and build the whole space, we plan the lighting with the hardscape so the wiring is clean and hidden.

Keep it low-voltage and durable

Quality low-voltage LED systems are energy-sipping, long-lasting, and safe. The difference between a system that looks great for years and one that fails is in the fixtures, the wiring, and the placement — which is where a design-build crew earns its keep.
