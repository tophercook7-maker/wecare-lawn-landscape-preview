Guides · Concrete Artistry

Custom sculpted concrete, explained

More sculpture than slab

Most concrete work is flat and functional. Custom sculpted concrete is different — it's shaped, carved, colored, and textured by hand into three-dimensional features: realistic boulders, stone formations, wood-inspired elements, and original architectural pieces designed for one specific spot in your yard.

How it's different from stamped concrete

Stamped concrete presses a repeating pattern into a flat pour. Sculpted concrete is built up and carved into real shapes and forms — closer to art than to a patio surface. It lets us control the exact scale, form, and character of a feature and blend it naturally into the surrounding landscape.

Why it matters

It's what lets us create something that natural stone or ready-made products simply can't, and it becomes the centerpiece people remember. It's the signature of a WeCare Landscapes landscape. See more on concrete artistry or explore design & build.
