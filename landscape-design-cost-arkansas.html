What does a landscape design-build project cost?

Why there's no single price

A landscape project is like a kitchen remodel — the range is wide because the scope is wide. A planting refresh is a different investment than a full backyard transformation with a patio, water feature, lighting, and custom concrete artistry. What drives cost is the stonework, the materials, the site conditions (grading, drainage, access), and the level of custom detail.

A realistic starting point

Most complete design-build projects begin around $5,000 and go up from there based on scope. The best way to know is a consultation: we walk your property, talk through your goals, and shape a plan that fits both your vision and your budget.

What you're paying for

With WeCare Landscapes, the person who designs your project is the person who builds it — owner-led, not subcontracted. You're paying for craftsmanship that lasts and a finished space that looks like it belongs to your home. Explore design & build or request a consultation.
