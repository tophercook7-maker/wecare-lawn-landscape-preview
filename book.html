How Consultations Work

Every property is different, and the right solution isn't always obvious at first. We start by understanding what you're experiencing, what you'd like to change, and what matters most to you.

When an on-site visit makes sense, we'll walk the property together, look at the conditions involved, and ask the right questions to better understand what may be causing the problem — or holding the space back.

From there, we'll explore your options together so you have a clearer understanding of what makes sense, what may not, and what the next step could look like.

On-site consultations in Hot Springs and Hot Springs Village are $125, fully credited toward your project when you move forward with WeCare. For properties outside our primary service area, consultation pricing is based on location.
