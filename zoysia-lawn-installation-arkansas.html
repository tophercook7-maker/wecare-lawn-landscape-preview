A new Zoysia lawn is one of the best upgrades you can make to a Central Arkansas property — dense, fine-bladed, and tough in our heat. Here's what a professional installation actually involves, and how WeCare includes the Zoysia free when we install your lawn, because we grow our own.

Why professional installation matters

Sod succeeds or fails in the first two weeks, and most of that is decided before a single piece is laid. A lawn that's rushed onto poorly prepared ground will show every low spot, seam, and weak root. Professional installation gets the foundation right — the grade, the soil contact, and the watering plan — so the lawn roots fast and knits into a seamless carpet.

What professional Zoysia installation includes

  • Site assessment & measurement — we confirm sun, drainage, and exactly how much sod your space needs.
  • Removing the old lawn & debris so the new sod sits on clean ground.
  • Grading & soil prep — smoothing the surface and correcting low spots so water drains and the lawn lies flat.
  • Laying fresh-cut Matrella Zoysia from our own farm, tightly seamed and staggered for a solid knit.
  • Rolling & finishing for firm soil contact — the key to fast rooting.
  • A clear first-30-days watering plan so you know exactly how to bring it in.

Is Zoysia right for your yard?

Zoysia is our top pick for many local lawns — it forms a thick, weed-crowding carpet, handles heat and humidity, needs less mowing than bermuda, and takes some light shade. It does go dormant (tan) in winter like other warm-season grasses. If your yard is deeply shaded or you want winter color, it's worth comparing your options first.
