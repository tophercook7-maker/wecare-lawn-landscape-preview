Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

We Care ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and assistant, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

We collect only what you share with us to serve your request, which may include your name, phone number, email address, property address, project details, photographs you send, and the messages you exchange with our online assistant (Sage) or our forms.

How we use it

  • To respond to your questions and requests
  • To prepare estimates, schedule consultations, and provide services
  • To contact you about your request by phone, text, or email
  • To keep records of our work with you

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it except with service providers that help us operate (for example, secure database and hosting providers) or when required by law.

Phone & text-message consent

When you give us your phone number through our website, forms, or assistant, you agree that We Care may contact you by phone call and text message about your request and services. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. Consent is not a condition of purchase. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, or ask us to stop contacting you by calling (501) 627-4384. Reply HELP for help.

How your information is stored

Submissions are stored in a secure, access-controlled database used to manage customer requests. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may request that we update or delete the information you've shared, or that we stop contacting you, by calling (501) 627-4384 or emailing derrick@wecarelandscapes.expert.
