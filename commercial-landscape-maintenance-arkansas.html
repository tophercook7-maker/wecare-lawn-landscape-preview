Commercial landscape maintenance in Central Arkansas: what to expect & what it costs

For a hotel, an HOA, an office park, or any business with a front door, the grounds make the impression before anyone walks inside. This guide covers what commercial landscape maintenance includes, how it's priced, and what separates dependable grounds care from a crew that shows up when it feels like it.

Your grounds are the first thing customers judge

Overgrown beds, brown turf, and litter tell visitors something about how a business is run — long before they meet anyone. Clean, consistent grounds do the opposite: they signal that the details are handled. For commercial properties, landscape maintenance isn't an expense so much as part of how the property presents itself every single day.

What commercial landscape maintenance includes

A commercial maintenance program is built around your property and scheduled so it always looks cared for. Depending on the site, it typically covers:

  • Mowing, edging & trimming on a set schedule, with clean lines and blown-off walks and lots.
  • Bed maintenance — weeding, mulching, and keeping plantings sharp.
  • Shrub & ornamental pruning to keep the property tidy and healthy.
  • Seasonal color & cleanups — spring and fall resets, leaf removal, and seasonal plantings where wanted.
  • Turf health — fertilization and weed control to keep the lawn thick and green.
  • Irrigation checks so problems get caught before the turf shows it.
  • Litter & general grounds policing so the property always looks maintained.

Who it's for

We maintain grounds for hotels and resorts, homeowners' associations and communities, medical and professional offices, retail and restaurants, and apartment and multi-family properties across the Hot Springs and Central Arkansas area. If people see it, it's worth keeping sharp.

How commercial maintenance is priced

There's no single sticker price — commercial maintenance is quoted per property. What shapes it is the size of the grounds, the scope of services, the visit frequency, and the level of detail the property calls for (a flagship hotel entrance is a different standard than a back lot). Most commercial accounts run on a recurring monthly agreement so budgeting is predictable and the property never falls behind. The right way to get a real number is a walk-through: we assess the property and put together a program and price that fit it.
