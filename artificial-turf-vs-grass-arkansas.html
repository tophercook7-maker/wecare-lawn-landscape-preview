Artificial turf vs. natural grass in Arkansas

The case for turf

Artificial turf gives you a clean, always-green lawn with no mowing, watering, or muddy patches. It shines in tough spots — heavy shade where grass won't grow, pet areas, play spaces, tight courtyards, and around pools and patios. The upfront cost is higher, but there's almost no ongoing upkeep.

The case for natural sod

Real grass is cooler underfoot, costs less to install, and — with the right variety like Matrella zoysia — looks and feels fantastic. It does need mowing, watering, and seasonal care.

Often, the answer is both

Many of the best yards use turf where grass struggles and natural sod everywhere else. The right call depends on your property, your budget, and how you use the space — which is exactly what we help you figure out. Learn about our turf work.
