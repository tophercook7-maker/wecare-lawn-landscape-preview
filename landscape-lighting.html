Good questions

Answers

Is landscape lighting energy efficient?

Yes — modern low-voltage LED systems use very little power and last for years, so you get the ambiance without a big electric bill.

Can you add lighting to an existing landscape?

Often yes — we can design a lighting layer for an established yard, or build it into a new landscape project from the start.

What can be lit?

Trees, stonework, walls, water features, concrete artistry, paths, steps, patios — we plan the layers so it looks natural, not floodlit.
