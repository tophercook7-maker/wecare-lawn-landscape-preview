About We Care

Three sides of your yard. One family that cares.

We're a family-run landscape company in the Hot Springs area, and we've been shaping Central Arkansas yards since 1998. Design and build, custom carved concrete, drainage, water features, our own sod farm, and year-round lawn care — all under one roof, all backed by the same crew and the same standard.

Our story

We Care started the way most good Arkansas businesses do — with a truck, a strong work ethic, and a promise to show up and do right by people. More than 25 years later, that hasn't changed. What's grown is what we can do for you: from a freshly mowed lawn to a full outdoor living space, we've built the skills and the crew to handle the whole property.

Owner Derrick Collier still leads every project personally. When you hire We Care, you're not handed off to a subcontractor you've never met — the person who designs your project is the person who builds it. That's rare, and it's the whole point.

What sets us apart

Carved concrete artistry

Plenty of companies can pour a patio. Our signature is different: we carve and sculpt concrete by hand, on site, into one-of-a-kind features — realistic tree stumps, faux rock, and custom fire and water elements you won't find anywhere else in the area. It's part craft, part art, and it's where We Care really stands apart. If you can picture it, there's a good chance we can build it in stone and concrete that lasts for decades.

One team, three divisions

Everything your property needs

We Care Luxury Landscapes

Custom design & build — outdoor living spaces, hardscape and stonework, pondless water features, drainage, irrigation, and landscape lighting.

We Care Concrete Artistry

Hand-carved, sculpted concrete features — our signature work, built right into your landscape.

We Care Lawn & Property Care

Reliable mowing, cleanups, seasonal care, and farm-fresh sod from our own fields, delivered and installed.

What you can count on

We hire for character first and teach the skills — so the people on your property are people we'd trust on our own. We keep you in the loop, we clean up after ourselves, and we treat your investment like it's our own yard. Fair prices, real craftsmanship, and a family name on every job.

Based in Hot Springs and Hot Springs Village, we serve the surrounding communities for lawn and sod, and we travel across Central Arkansas — Benton, Bryant, Little Rock, Conway, and Russellville — for design-build and concrete projects.
