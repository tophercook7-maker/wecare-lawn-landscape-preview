About WeCare Landscapes

Where landscape craftsmanship meets artistry

A well-designed landscape should look as though it belongs to the property — not as though it was assembled from a catalog.

WeCare Landscapes is a family-owned landscape company creating distinctive outdoor spaces through thoughtful design, skilled construction, and close attention to detail. Our work includes traditional masonry, natural stonework, artificial turf, landscape lighting, water features, drainage solutions, planting, sod, and ongoing property care.

What sets us apart

Custom sculpted concrete

What gives us greater creative freedom is our expertise in custom sculpted concrete. We view concrete as an artistic medium — not simply a construction material. It can be shaped, carved, colored, and textured to create boulders, stone formations, wood-inspired features, and original architectural elements designed for a particular setting. This allows us to control the scale, form, and character of a feature while integrating it naturally into the surrounding landscape.

Traditional materials remain an important part of our work. Custom concrete artistry simply expands what is possible when natural stone or ready-made products cannot fully accomplish the design.

Our roots

It started in 1998

Our roots go back to 1998, when founder Derrick Collier transformed a neglected property filled with rocks and debris into one of the most attractive landscapes in its neighborhood. That first project established an approach that continues today: understand the property, recognize its potential, and create something that feels both beautiful and intentional.

Today WeCare Landscapes is family-owned and operated — Derrick's wife runs the administrative side, his daughter helps with social media, and his sons work in the field.

The standard is in our name

We believe artistry should never come at the expense of function or customer experience. Our clients deserve creative ideas, practical solutions, honest recommendations, dependable communication, and respect for their property.

The name WeCare Landscapes holds us accountable to that promise. The finished work should demonstrate not only what we can create, but how seriously we take the people who trust us to create it.

At WeCare Landscapes, the standard is in our name.
