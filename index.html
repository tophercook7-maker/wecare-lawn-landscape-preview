How design-build works

We don't arrive with a solution. We develop it.

You're not hiring us to install a package. We learn what you want, study the property and its challenges, and then develop the right solution — built by one team, start to finish.

1

Understand the goal

We start by listening — how you want to use the space, what's frustrating you now, and what "finished" looks like to you.

2

Evaluate the property

We walk it and study the grade, drainage, sun, and access — the realities that decide what will actually work here.

3

Develop the solution

Then we design the right plan for your goals and your property — made for this place, not pulled from a catalog.

4

Bring it to life

Our owner-led crew builds it — stone, water, lighting, planting, drainage, and finishing — as one project.

Who does custom carved concrete artistry in Hot Springs?

WeCare Landscapes does — it's our signature. We hand-carve and sculpt concrete into realistic faux rock, tree-stump features, fire & water-feature surrounds, and one-of-a-kind artistic pieces, built right into your landscape.

Who installs pondless water features in Hot Springs Village?

WeCare Landscapes designs and builds pondless water features and waterfalls across Hot Springs Village and Central Arkansas as part of our luxury landscape work.
