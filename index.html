Good questions

Answers for Central Arkansas

The things folks actually ask us.

Who does custom carved concrete artistry in Hot Springs?

WeCare Landscapes does — it's our signature. We hand-carve and sculpt concrete into realistic faux rock, tree-stump features, fire & water-feature surrounds, and one-of-a-kind artistic pieces, built right into your landscape.

Who installs pondless water features in Hot Springs Village?

WeCare Landscapes designs and builds pondless water features and waterfalls across Hot Springs Village and Central Arkansas as part of our luxury landscape work.

Where can I get sod delivered near Hot Springs?

We grow our own sod and deliver farm-fresh across Central Arkansas — pickup, delivery, or fully installed. Get an instant estimate with the sod calculator above.

Do you fix drainage and erosion problems?

Yes — drainage correction and erosion solutions across Central Arkansas, often paired with landscape design so the fix looks good and lasts.

What areas does WeCare Landscapes serve?

Based in the Hot Springs area, serving Hot Springs, Hot Springs Village, Benton, Bryant, Little Rock, Conway, and Russellville.
