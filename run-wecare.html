How to Run WeCare

A quick guide for Derrick & Brandi — from login to running the day.

Everything lives behind one link: wecarelandscapes.expert/apps.html. Bookmark it. Here's how the pieces fit together.

1

🔐 Log into the office

The first time you open any office tool (Owner Home, Leads, Field Ops), it asks you to create an office password — you and Brandi pick it together. After that, it's just enter-the-password.

  • Your crew never needs this password — they use their own PIN.
  • You can change the password anytime from the login screen.
Open the office →
2

👷 Get your crew set up

Each crew member sets up their own account — you just approve them.

  • Send each person this link: wecarelandscapes.expert/crew-signup.html
  • They fill in their info, W-2/payroll details (last-4 of SSN only — the full W-4 you do on paper), and pick a 4-digit clock-in PIN.
  • They show up in Field Ops → 👥 Team marked Pending. Tap ✅ Approve and they can clock in.
💡 You can print each person's profile for your paper file, and reset a PIN anytime from the Team panel.
Open Field Ops →See the sign-up form
3

🗓️ Run your day — start at Owner Home

Owner Home is your morning glance: what's scheduled today, and colored chips for anything that needs you (follow-ups due, new leads, booking requests). Green ✓ means you're caught up.

Owner Home →
4

📇 Work your leads & bookings

  • Leads / CRM — every call, form, and Sage chat lands here. Move each one New → Contacted → Quoted → Won. It tells you who's due for a follow-up and writes the text for you.
  • Consultations — when someone books, tap Text to confirm, and 🚗 On my way when you're headed there.
Leads / CRM →
5

🧰 Assign work & track the money

In Field Ops you create work orders and assign your crew — the job shows up on their phones. When they clock in and you enter what you charged, you see your real profit per job. Payroll totals up automatically (Chris's day rate handled for you); tap ⬇ CSV when it's time to pay.

Field Ops →
6

📱 What your crew sees

Once approved, each crew member opens the Crew app, taps their name, enters their PIN, and: picks today's job, clocks in (GPS-stamped), follows the step-by-step checklist, and snaps before/after photos — all of which flow straight back to you in Field Ops.

See the Crew app →
🌱 Sage (the green assistant on your website) works 24/7 — it answers customers, quotes sod, and books consultations right into your Leads. You'll always see every chat and can jump in anytime.
