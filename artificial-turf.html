Good questions

Answers

Does artificial turf drain well?

Yes — with the right base and installation, water drains through the turf just like a natural lawn. Proper prep is the difference between turf that lasts and turf that puddles.

Is it good for pets?

It is. Pet-friendly turf with the right base and infill rinses clean and holds up to regular use.

Can turf be part of a bigger landscape project?

Absolutely — we design turf into the whole plan alongside stonework, planting, drainage, and lighting so it looks like it belongs.
