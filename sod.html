Watering & prep guidance

  • Before delivery: kill or remove old grass and weeds, then grade and lightly loosen the soil so it's smooth and level.
  • Lay it fresh: sod is perishable — plan to install within a day of delivery, in a brick-stagger pattern with tight seams.
  • Water right away: soak thoroughly within 30 minutes of laying, then keep it consistently moist for the first 2–3 weeks while it roots.
  • First mow: wait until the sod is firmly rooted (about 2–3 weeks) before the first cut.
