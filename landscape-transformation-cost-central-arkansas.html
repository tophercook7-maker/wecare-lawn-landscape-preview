What does a $25,000–$50,000+ landscape transformation look like in Central Arkansas?

If you've started pricing a retaining wall, a drainage fix, or a new patio, you may already sense the bigger picture: the parts of your property that aren't working are usually connected. A complete landscape transformation solves them together — and turns a property you tolerate into one you actually want to live in.

Here's what a project at this level involves in the Hot Springs and Central Arkansas area, what drives the investment, and why doing it as one designed project is worth it.

A real WeCare transformation — see the full project in Our Work →

A transformation isn't one service — it's several working as one

The reason these projects reach $25,000 to $50,000 and beyond is that they combine multiple trades that would each be its own job. Done together, they're designed to fit — the drainage disappears into the grading, the retaining wall becomes part of the terracing, the lighting was planned before the beds went in. A complete project usually includes some mix of:

  • Grading & site preparation

    Reshaping the land so water goes where it should and the whole design sits right. Everything else is built on this.

  • Drainage & erosion correction

    Solving standing water, runoff, and washouts so the finished landscape lasts. Often the first problem people call about. How we fix a soggy yard →

  • Retaining walls & terracing

    Turning a slope into usable, level space — structural and beautiful at once, and one of the highest-impact parts of a transformation. When you need a retaining wall & what they cost →

  • Hardscaping — patios, walkways & stone

    Flagstone, pavers, and natural stone that give the space its structure and where you actually live outdoors. Paver vs. concrete patio →

  • Custom concrete artistry

    WeCare's signature — hand-sculpted boulders, stone formations, and features you can't buy off a shelf. A true differentiator that raises the whole project. What custom concrete artistry is →

  • Planting & bed renovation

    Trees, shrubs, and beds chosen for this site and this climate, arranged as part of the design rather than added on.

  • New sod — and WeCare grows its own Zoysia

    A finished lawn ties the whole space together. Because we grow our own Zoysia, the sod is free with professional installation. Free Zoysia with installation →

  • Landscape lighting

    Warm, low-voltage lighting that makes the space usable and striking after dark — planned into the design from the start. Landscape lighting ideas →

  • Outdoor & water features

    Pondless waterfalls, fire features, and focal points that give a property its character. About pondless water features →

Why doing it as one project is worth it

You could hire six different contractors over three years. But piecemeal work almost always costs more and looks it — a wall here, a patio there, none of it designed to belong together. Building it as one project means one design vision, one team, one mobilization, and details that line up: the drainage is solved under the patio, the wall is sized for the terracing, the lighting is run before the sod goes down. You get a finished environment, not a collection of separate jobs.

What drives the $25K–$50K+ range

Every property is different, but the investment is shaped by a few things: the amount of stonework and the materials chosen, site conditions like grade, drainage, and access, the scale of the space, and the level of custom detail — especially custom concrete features. A consultation is how we turn those variables into a real plan and a real number.

Maybe you're starting with one problem

A lot of our clients first reached out about a single thing — a wall, a wet backyard, a patch of lawn that won't grow. Then they saw what the whole property could become and decided to do it right, once. If that sounds like you, a consultation is the place to explore it — no pressure, just a plan.
